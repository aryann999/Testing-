01 / STUDIO

We design things people interact with, live with and remember.

TAURI operates between industrial design, product development, CGI, packaging and brand experience.

We don't believe in designing inside categories. A product can be a machine, an object, a system, a story or all of them at once.

Our job is to find the idea at the centre and make it impossible to ignore.
